How the Quizzes Work

Answering is only half of it. The explanation after each question is where the learning happens.

One question at a time

No rushing ahead. Each question is answered and explained before the next one appears.

Instant feedback

The correct option is highlighted straight away, with the reasoning underneath it.

Timer when you want it

Practise calmly with the timer off, or add pressure once a topic feels familiar.

Full review at the end

Every question comes back with your answer next to the correct one.

Getting More Out of a Quiz

A few habits that turn a score into real understanding.

Predict before you click

Say the answer in your head first. If you cannot, that topic is worth revisiting.

Repeat the ones you missed

Run the same quiz again after a simulation. The gap usually closes quickly.

Explain it out loud

If you can explain why an answer is right to a classmate, you understand it.
