What is Biology?

Biology is the study of living things and how they grow, survive, reproduce and interact with their environment.

Living Things

Plants, animals, humans and microorganisms are examples of living things.

Cells

Cells are the basic units that make up living organisms.

Life Processes

Living things carry out processes such as nutrition, respiration, growth and reproduction.

Life Processes

Scientists often remember seven life processes. Here they are in everyday language.

Movement

Living things can move themselves or parts of themselves.

Example: You walk. A plant grows towards light.

Respiration

Cells release energy from food so the organism can stay alive.

Example: Your cells use oxygen to help release energy from glucose.

Sensitivity

Living things detect and respond to changes around them.

Example: You pull your hand away from something hot.

Growth

Living things increase in size and develop over time.

Example: A seedling becomes a taller plant.

Reproduction

Living things produce offspring of the same kind.

Example: Flowering plants make seeds. Animals have young.

Excretion

Living things remove waste produced by their cells.

Example: You breathe out carbon dioxide. You also produce urine.

Nutrition

Living things take in materials they need for energy and growth.

Example: You eat food. Plants make glucose using light.
