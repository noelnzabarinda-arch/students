    Smart Science Study Tips

    Five habits that make every topic easier to understand.

    1

    Read carefully

    Read the concept slowly before doing anything else.

    2

    Study the diagrams

    Look at the pictures and examples that go with it.

    3

    Try the activity

    Use the interactive activity to see the idea in action.

    4

    Test yourself

    Answer questions without looking at your notes.

    5

    Review again

    Go back to the difficult topics a second time.
