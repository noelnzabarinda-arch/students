What Did You Observe?

  • What changed when you increased the force?
  • What happened when the mass increased?
  • What happened when temperature increased in the particle lab?
  • What happened to particles in a gas compared to a solid?
  • What happens to the lungs during inhalation?
Increasing force increases acceleration (a = F/m), so the object speeds up faster. Increasing mass makes it harder to accelerate for the same force. Higher temperature makes particles move faster and spread out — that's why gases expand more than liquids or solids. During inhalation, the diaphragm contracts and moves down, the lungs expand, and air flows in.
