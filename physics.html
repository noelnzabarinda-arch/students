What is Physics?

Physics helps us understand how objects move, how forces act, how gravity pulls things toward Earth, how pressure works and how energy changes from one form to another.

Force

Makes an object push, pull or change its motion.

Motion

Describes how an object changes position with time.

Energy

Allows things to move or cause changes.

Can You Solve It?

Use what you've learned about force and acceleration.

A box has a mass of 5 kg. A force of 20 N acts on it. What is its acceleration?
a = F ÷ m
a = 20 ÷ 5
a = 4 m/s²

Did You Know?

A force can make an object start moving, stop moving, speed up, slow down or change direction.
